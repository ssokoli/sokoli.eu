## Current

Since 2012 I teach **Spanish Language (ΙΣΠ10, Level I)** in the undergraduate programme "Spanish Language and Culture" of the **Hellenic Open University (ΕΑΠ)**, where I also taught Levels I and II in 2006–2008.

In December 2025 I taught at the **Universidad Nacional de Rosario**, Argentina, in the Diplomatura de estudios avanzados en herramientas teóricas y técnicas aplicadas a la traducción audiovisual.

## Postgraduate programmes

From 2012 to 2022 I taught **"La subtitulación según los medios de comunicación"** in the Master en Traducción Literaria y Audiovisual at the **Universitat Pompeu Fabra**, Barcelona.

From 2000 to 2016 I taught in the postgraduate programme **"Tradumática: Traducción y Nuevas Tecnologías"** at the **Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona**: *El entorno de trabajo del traductor*, *Internet para traductores*, and *Herramientas de subtitulación*.

## Teaching innovation programmes (UNED)

Distance teaching within UNED teaching-innovation projects: **Audiosub** (audio description and SDH for language learning, 2019–2021), **Doblaje y subtitulación creativa** (2017–2018), **Sub-Lite** (SDH for listening and writing skills, 2015–2016), and **iDub** (intralingual dubbing for oral skills, 2014–2015), plus the 2015 summer course *La Traducción Audiovisual y el Aprendizaje de Idiomas* in Madrid.

## Professional training

Distance and in-person subtitling courses (English–Greek) at the **meta|φραση School of Translation Studies**, Athens: 2005–2008 and 2015–2021.

## Research in the classroom

My current empirical work on AI and student design work (see [Research](/research/)) is based on a study conducted in the ΕΑΠ course ΓΤΠ51.

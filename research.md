My publications, the most recent first. They are about four subjects:

- how subtitles are written, and what they leave out
- how films with more than one language are translated
- subtitling and revoicing as activities for learning a language
- since 2025, artificial intelligence in education and audiovisual creation

My doctoral thesis, *Subtitling Norms in Greece and Spain* (Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, 2011), compares the two countries on two points: what film subtitles omit, and how the dialogue is divided into subtitles.

Each title links to the publisher's page, or to the full text when it is free to read. See also my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Q5VrkMAAAAAJ&hl=en) and [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2970-8729) pages, and the [research projects](/projects/).

<p>Tech people obsess about ease of use. But #Zoom’s ease was good until it wasn’t. <br>
Requiring everyone to enter a password to join a meeting, or making a teacher approve 30 students sharing their homework is a pain. It’s also protection against Zoom-crashing.</p>

<p>by Shira Ovide, NYT.<br>
We shouldn’t just continue to use its product just because it works well and is simple to use. There are protective steps you can take if you need to use Zoom, but there are alternatives such as FaceTime and Webex.</p>

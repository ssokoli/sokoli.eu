<p>Windows device: select Start and type “Narrator.” Narrator includes a first-run walkthrough to help you along.</p>
<p>Apple device: turn on the VoiceOver screen reader in Settingsunder General &gt; Accessibility.</p>
<p>Android’s TalkBack screen reader; go to Settings&gt;Accessibility &gt;Talkback.</p>

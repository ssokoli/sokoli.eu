<p>Some #subtitlers omit the "wow"s found in English templates, but not all do. Newbies especially are too worried they'll get low scores due to "omission"😖. Netflix is doing serious damage to subtitling with those metrics. Not per se, but because of their appalling use &amp; misuse <a href="https://twitter.com/carol_osullivan/status/1174966623603552264">https://twitter.com/carol_osullivan/status/1174966623603552264</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Including exclamations and other recoverable expressions does no service to accessibility. #Deaf and HoH viewers need real #SDH with all relevant sound descriptions. Not half-baked solutions. Reading some "wow"s is not of much help when all important nonverbal info is missing.</p>

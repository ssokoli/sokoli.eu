<p>Shall we keep in mind when using the term #ResponsibleAI that #AI cannot actually be responsible? That it can only be *used* responsibly?<br>
Sure, it’s a shortcut people use to emphasize the collective responsibility for ethical use, minimizing harm, maximizing fairness, transparency, accountability.<br>
But it’s worth reminding ourselves now and then that the responsibility lies with us.</p>

<p>We have been talking about "read-along subtitles" (like #SDH but without sound descriptors) used for dubbed content (hence the name #dubtitles). But why not pair them with the original audio too? A fantastic idea by #Netflix, catering to the needs of so many hearing viewers who enjoy subtitles:<br>
- language learners<br>
- individuals with ADD or divided attention (hey, multitaskers)<br>
- people in noisy environments (looking at you, crunchy snacks)<br>
- those needing silence (anybody with sleeping partners and/or kids?)<br>
- young viewers who can’t get enough of subtitles for one reason or another<br>
- film enthusiasts who don’t want to miss a single word - especially Christopher Nolan fans 😉</p>
<p>It was great to see some of these categories highlighted by Mark S Harrison in his presentation at Languages &amp; The Media #LAM24, as I had also expanded on them during my talk at #HispaTAV in Madrid.</p>
<p> #intralingualsubtitles</p>

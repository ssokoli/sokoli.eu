<p>Have you noticed more and more paid mentorship courses, and program-style offerings for translators and linguists lately on LI?</p>
<p>At the same time, what we hear constantly is that rates are becoming unsustainable, people are leaving the profession, the future is uncertain. And yet, this seems to be a moment where these programs are multiplying.</p>
<p>I’m genuinely trying to understand. Is it demand? Are people looking for support in a shaky moment? Or is it supply? Experienced professionals finally having the time and tools (AI included) to turn their experience into programs?</p>
<p>And I wonder about affordability (favourite word, right?) When something costs the equivalent of one or several months of a freelancer’s income, it’s hard not to wonder who these offerings are really for.</p>
<p>No conclusions on my part here. Just questions.</p>

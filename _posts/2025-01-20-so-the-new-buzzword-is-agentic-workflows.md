<p>So, the new buzzword is #agentic workflows'? What I see labeled as such in most cases is just a dressed-up #automation pipeline. Linear #prompts chained together with a bit of decision logic.</p>
<p>According to experts, a AI agent is autonomous, adaptive, and proactive, it can learn, evolve, handle diverse and unpredictable situations.</p>
<p>Can we stop inflating simple automation with AI buzzwords? Probably not. But if you do, just know that people will notice. And judge, even if they don’t say it out loud. People aren’t stupid.</p>

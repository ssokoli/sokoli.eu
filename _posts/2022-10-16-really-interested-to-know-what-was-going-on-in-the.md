<p>Really interested to know what was going on in the mind of the researchers who came up with the term #hallucinations for #NMT results that are completely decoupled from the input sentence.<br>
<a href="https://openreview.net/pdf?id=SJxTk3vB3m">https://openreview.net/pdf?id=SJxTk3vB3m</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>So, #hallucination was a term first used by researchers working with NMT to mean "untethered from source" but came to mean "untethered from reality" when used by GPT researchers and then users.<br>
Alternative terms include "bullshit", "lies" and less loaded "factual inaccuracies".</p>

<p>In 2013 the #blind community started asking Netflix for #accessible content. Technology was prepared in 2014 (styleguides etc). In the spring 2015 Daredevil was launched, at first without AD (a shame, as the character is blind) 2015=100 shows. Soon all Netflix Originals were ADed</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Some TV programmes that have been #audiodescribed before are not available on Netflix with AD and that has to do with licencing content. Sometimes the sub-licenses do not know that there is AD. It's difficult for Netflix to know where the AD is and how to have access to it.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>As for the future, synthesized voice technology will improve. Listening to Erica Kram on Accessible Netflix #RNIBConnect #TechTalk <a href="http://audioboom.com/posts/7057032-accessible-netflix-and-emails">http://audioboom.com/posts/7057032-accessible-netflix-and-emails</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>The presenters comment on synthesized voices looking at advantages (cheaper, faster) but also wondering whether emotion would be lost. But then again, should there be any emotion in #audiodescription? Not sure whether there should be a bit. But eg. there is none in House of Cards</p>
{% endraw %}

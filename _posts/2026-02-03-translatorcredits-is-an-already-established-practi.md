<p>#TranslatorCredits is an already established practice that signals #humanmade translation, even if it wasn't its original purpose.</p>
<p>My prediction is that this logic is going to be extended to all human made content, especially as AI-generated content surpasses it. So, instead of marking what’s made by AI (see CR marks on top left corner of AI images), we’ll start marking what’s made by humans. Like EXIF data in digital photos.</p>

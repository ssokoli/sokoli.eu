<p>#AIinEducation<br>
With the advanced reasoning capabilities of AI (see OpenAI's brand new #DeepSearch), #flipped classrooms seem the best way forward. No take-home projects. Students engage in critical thinking and problem-solving during class.</p>
<p>As Ethan Mollick, author of Co-Intelligence: Living and Working with AI, says in a comment: "Sometimes you have to learn to do things the hard way so you can understand what you are doing when you have tools. We don't expect audiobooks to replace reading or calculators to replace the ability to understand math. We want educated humans."</p>
<p> #TheEndofHomework</p>

<p>I am increasingly convinced that the actual implementation of metrics in #subtitling may have a detrimental effect on quality. Looking at mediocre subtitle files with excellent metrics and work with imagination and fluency being punished for technicalities. It's depressing.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>And why would such an implementation be bad for quality? If you're a subtitler and you have x time for a task, where would you place your priorities? Delivering a technically excellent file but with mediocre, literal (but not wrong) renderings? Or spend time working on the words?</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Yeah, I know, a good subtitler should do all of that in no time. And if the deadline's too tight they should refuse work. Right...</p>
{% endraw %}

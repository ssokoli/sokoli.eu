<p>It seems that the Chinese Ministry of Education has adopted a tiered approach to GenAI use in schools:<br>
-Primary school students are prohibited from using unrestricted GenAI tools on their own<br>
-Middle schoolers can explore how GenAI reasons and analyzes information,<br>
-High schoolers are allowed to use the tech more broadly</p>
<p>Information comes from a local report about the Ministry's newly published guidelines.<br>

<p>#AVT people, have in mind that things like KNP (Key Names and Phrases) etc is Netflix terminology, introduced by them in 2017. It didn't exist in the industry before.</p>
<p>I tried to find if it was used before in other contexts, and got only a few cases mainly in religious texts. <a href="https://twitter.com/AvtMasterclass/status/1687705122988568578">https://twitter.com/AvtMasterclass/status/1687705122988568578</a></p>

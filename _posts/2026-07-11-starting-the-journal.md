A subtitler's whole job is timing: deciding how long a line can sit on screen before it either outstays the shot or leaves the viewer behind. This journal is the same problem in a different register, a place for the paragraphs that don't fit in a paper or a syllabus, but that I don't want to leave un-timed either.

Expect notes on translation, on teaching, on football when it earns the space, and on whatever else survives a second read. In English, in español, and en català, depending on what the thought was thinking in.

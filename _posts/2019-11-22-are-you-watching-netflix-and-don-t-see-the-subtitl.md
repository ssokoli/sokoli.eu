<p>Are you watching #Netflix and don't see the #subtitles in the language you want? It does not mean they are not there; the app shows the "relevant" ones (location, preferences etc). Two ways to check: <br>
1. Create a profile and set it in that language (Account&gt;My Profile&gt;Language)</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>2. Try the Chrome extension Language Learning with #Netflix.   You will see all available #subtitles in the menu, not just the 3-4 that appear by default in your region. Once you start using a specific language on your desktop it will appear as an option on other devices too.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Same for audio, of course. You can see all the #dubbing and #audiodescription options available. Really cool combinations you can do, all you #multilinguals out there</p>
{% endraw %}

<p>[Off topic]</p>
<p>I hate running.</p>
<p>Give me cycling, swimming, canoeing, hiking - anything but running and its constant pounding on my old bones.</p>
<p>That’s why I’m so proud of my first participation in a race: the #PatrasHalfMarathon. I did the 5km, of course, not the 21km.</p>
<p>Colleagues from Computer Technology Institute and Press “DIOPHANTUS” (CTI) and family decided to participate as a group, and I couldn’t fall behind. I trained 3-4 times, only to confirm how much I dislike it, and to start worrying whether I could finish in under 50 minutes.</p>
<p>But running with other people is different. You look around and see amateurs like you, struggling, but somehow lifting each other up.<br>
And then you hit the last 200m. A crowd is cheering. You feel the adrenaline, your whole body tingling, and you give it a final push - even though you thought it didn’t matter if you came last. (Btw, I hate adrenaline rushes, but this one was amazing - like a drug.)</p>
<p>40 minutes. Medal says "finisher".</p>
<p>No way I can do that time again outside a race. But I’m already looking forward to the next one. I might even train for it.</p>
<p>PS: The organization was impeccable - totally recommended.</p>

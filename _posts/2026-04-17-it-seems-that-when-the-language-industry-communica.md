<p>It seems that when the Language Industry communicates internally (in conferences) and externally (in publications or posts like this) only in English, it operates as an English-language Industry. How can we make it multilingual? How can we walk that talk?</p>
<p>It's a topic worth discussing, and I think the AI Localization Think Tank is a good space for it.</p>
<p>¿Hasta qué punto es creíble vender comunicación multilingüe y traducción cuando operamos y nos proyectamos de forma monolingüe, exclusivamente en inglés?</p>

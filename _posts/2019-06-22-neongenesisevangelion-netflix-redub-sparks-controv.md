<p>If the English redub story was interesting, the Italian is fascinating. Dub writer #Cannarsi has been fiercely criticised for his literal rendering. Netflix chose the expert in japanese anime for Italian but the backlash was enough to bring it down. An re-redub is being made now.</p>

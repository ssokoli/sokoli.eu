<p>Anybody knows why #Netflix does not use audio introduction with film #audiodescription? Is it a technical issue? Is it considered unnecessary?</p>

<p>A woman with light brown hair removes a syringe from a vial. Nearby a woman with brown skin lies in a bathtub. Looking in a floor mirror the brown haired woman sees the reflection of the blond.<br>
In the derelict church, the brown-haired woman stares at the blond.</p>

<p>Evening. The platinum blond stares at the blond on the roof.<br>
Daytime. The woman with chin length hair, eyes wide. The blond stands in a pool of light.</p>

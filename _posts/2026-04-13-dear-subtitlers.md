<p>Dear subtitlers,</p>
<p>Sign your work, fill in that translator credit box, do not waive this right - it's not a given. Use your real name and maintain a public presence. Talk about your work (without breaking NDAs of course).</p>
<p>It has always been important on an individual level. But in the age of AI, it matters collectively, for the entire profession.</p>
<p>Translation should be invisible, not translators.</p>

<p>"The translator job is a big bullshit", taken out of context, can only mean a few things.</p>
<p>But in the mouth of the football coach José Mourinho, it means that the label "translator" to describe his job was pure nonsense. Haters would call him “the translator” to diminish the kind of work he did as assistant coach. Interpreting for head coach Bobby Robson was one of his tasks, but he was also an analyst, scout, goalkeeping coach, etc.</p>
<p>Being a long-time Barça fan helped me see this clearly and dodge a potential bullet when I subtitled the show. But even if I didn’t know the backstory, I would stop before translating such a “simple” phrase. Why would Mourinho say that out of the blue? What could he mean? This refers to actual facts, so an internet search would help. But, most importantly, later in the dialogue there are several hints that explain the true meaning of the phrase.</p>
<p>After clarifying the meaning, I had to decide how to translate it. Keep the ambiguity? Go for the speaker’s intent? I chose the latter, also taking into account that English is not his native language: "Είναι μπούρδες ότι δούλευα ως μεταφραστής" [It is bullshit/nonsense that I worked as a translator].</p>
<p>Anyways, #Mourinho by #Netflix is worth watching, and I'd say it's more about a controversial personality, his grit, tenacity and hard work, someone you either love or hate - than about football itself.</p>

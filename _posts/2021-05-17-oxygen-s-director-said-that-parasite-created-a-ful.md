<p>#Oxygen's director said that #Parasite “created a full, different world” for international filmmakers. <p>Now, according to the filmmaker, there is a greater sense of freedom in terms of respecting the language of each story.<br>
“It doesn’t have to be in English. You can stay ambitious and imagine that they will be released in other countries, even if they are in a different language.”</p>

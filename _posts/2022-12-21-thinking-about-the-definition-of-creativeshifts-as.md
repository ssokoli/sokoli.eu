<p>Thinking about the definition of #CreativeShifts as a central aspect of the notion of #TranslationalCreativity (departing from the ST structure by abstracting, modifying or concretising ideas) and how it relates to #NMT hallucinations. </p>
<p><a href="https://www.erudit.org/en/journals/meta/1900-v1-n1-meta043/1008339ar/">https://www.erudit.org/en/journals/meta/1900-v1-n1-meta043/1008339ar/</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>@AnaGuerberof and @_atoral take this into consideration by emphasizing the role of acceptability: "creativity combines acceptability (i.e. something of value, fit for purpose) and novelty (i.e. new, original"<br>
<a href="https://arxiv.org/ftp/arxiv/papers/2204/2204.05655.pdf">https://arxiv.org/ftp/arxiv/papers/2204/2204.05655.pdf</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Both notions of acceptability and novelty are tricky, especially in #subtitling. E.g. a creative solution may be acceptable to the translator community (or praised as in #SubtítulosConCarácter) but questioned by viewers with some understanding the ST (see #SquidGame critics).</p>

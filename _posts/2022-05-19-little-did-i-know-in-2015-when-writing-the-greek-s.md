<p>Little did I know in 2015 when writing the Greek subtitles for #Daredevil that it would be the first show to be offered with #AD. <br>
It was only the start of #Netflix's commitment to improve #accessibility <br>
#GAAD2022 <a href="https://www.theverge.com/2015/4/14/8413569/netflix-daredevil-audio-descriptions-now-available">https://www.theverge.com/2015/4/14/8413569/netflix-daredevil-audio-descriptions-now-available</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>On October 15, 2014, DRA transmitted a letter on behalf of the American Council of the Blind, the Bay State Council of the Blind, and Robert Baran to Netflix expressing concerns regarding the screen-reader #accessibility of Netflix’s website and apps,<br>
<a href="https://dralegal.org/case/netflix-settlement/">https://dralegal.org/case/netflix-settlement/</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>and the availability of audio description services.<br>
Claimants and Netflix determined that it was in their mutual best interests to resolve all potential claims and disputes between them.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>To avoid the costs and uncertainty of litigation, the parties  negotiated improvements to the Netflix website and mobile applications, and negotiated increased availability of videos with audio description in Netflix’s video streaming and disc rental libraries.</p>

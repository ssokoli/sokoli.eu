<p>What do directors think about #omission in #subtitles? It can be seen indirectly in how they use subtitles for foreign utterances in their films. Sometimes they omit them because their meaning can be retrieved regardless. E.g. somebody screams insults in another language. #L3AVT</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Such a decision implies that the actual words of the insult are not the priority. The creative intent is for the viewer to focus on the image and the sound, the facial expression and the tone of the voice, not the verbal part on the lower part of the screen.</p>

<p>Audiences are shaping the media production - and localisation - industry. If they find a new technique jarring, they stop watching. In this case, synthetic faces and voices are used in a documentary to anonymise victims. The AI use is transparent. But emotions are an issue, and viewers quit watching. If the numbers are high, the practice will be abandoned or changed.</p>

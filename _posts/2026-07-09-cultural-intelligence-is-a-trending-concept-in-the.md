<p>Cultural intelligence is a trending concept in the post-AI localization world, but it's at least 25 years old. An established field of research in cross-cultural psychology and international management.</p>
<p>For those interested in its origins and theoretical foundations, Cultural Intelligence: Individual Interactions Across Cultures by Christopher Earley and Soon Ang 2003 is an excellent place to start.</p>
<p>Long before AI made cultural intelligence fashionable, translators were putting it into practice every day.<br>
It's a pity that contributions of Translation Studies haven't received much attention in the mainstream CQ literature.</p>

<p>Celebrating 40 years of Computer Technology Institute and Press “DIOPHANTUS” (CTI) attending the last session, with Srini Devadas from the MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL), Richard DeMillo from Georgia Institute of Technology and Yiannis Kanellopoulos from code4thought, chaired by the president Dimitrios Serpanos, about security and privacy in the age of AI.</p>
<p>A very informative and inspiring session, just what we needed after one of my favourite ones, honouring the people forming the past, present and future of this great organisation, chaired by the poet-photographer-pianist Vasilis Delis. Lots of emotions there!</p>
<p>With Konstantinos Gratsias, Marina Sakouli, Elina Megalou, Dimitra Eggarchou, Maria Fountana, Nikolina Renieri, Christina Dimopoulou, Anisiou Eleni, Efstathiadou Rozina, Thanasis Hadzilacos, Dr Spyros Papadakis, Dimitris Kalles, Michalis Voukelatos, Giannis Siachos and so many other past and present colleagues.</p>

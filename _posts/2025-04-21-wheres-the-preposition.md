<p>Where’s the preposition?</p>
<p>We spent a bit of time debating it: AI in Localization? AI for Localization? Each option placed the focus in a different direction. Were we here to analyze, to advocate, to build, to question?</p>
<p>In the end, we dropped it.</p>
<p>Because part of what we wanted was to ask the right questions and bring together people who can help answer them, questions like:<br>
What does AI actually change in localization?<br>
Where does it disrupt, and for whom?<br>
What do we need to understand, technically, ethically, practically?<br>
How can we help ourselves and others?</p>
<p>I’m thrilled to be joining the AI Localization Think Tank, a space built not on a single message or party line, but on diversity of thought, where each expert brings their own experience, their own questions.</p>
<p>What we do have in common is our values: transparency, knowledge, kindness, understanding.</p>
<p>The group brings together top minds in the field and I'm just honored to be at the table, learning from them and contributing where I can.</p>

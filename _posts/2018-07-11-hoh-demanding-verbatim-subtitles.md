<p>#HoH demanding #verbatim #subtitles. The thread is interesting in itself, especially the different uses of #captions (not only for the #Deaf and HoH) <a href="https://twitter.com/MortuaryReport/status/1016808980134297600">https://twitter.com/MortuaryReport/status/1016808980134297600</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>#HoH viewers were not convinced by #Netflix argument that "Truncating the original dialogue is limited to instances where reading speed and synchronicity with the audio are an issue." They say they can read fast</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>#Readingspeed is relative. Not only depends on viewer's individual skills but also on content. With a show like @QueerEye, where dialogue is more important than image, verbatim rendering mmakes sense. In other content (films) where image is crucial, condensing is needed</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Partially #HoH viewers compare audio with #captions and feel cheated. Foreign language content subtitle consumers living in #subtitling countries might be more used to condensed #subtitles and do not seem to complain so strongly.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>But I have been asked, especially by viewers fluent in English,  why audio does not match #subtitles. When I explain the time and space restrictions, and the importance of the image, they seem convinced.</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>#Netflix #subtitles allow members to enjoy content without the need for silence or headphones. They are paramount also for the consumption of media in a world without the living room as the primary center of entertainment. <a href="https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/214969868-Why-are-Netflix-s-standards-for-Subtitles-and-Closed-Captions-so-high-">https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/214969868-Why-are-Netflix-s-standards-for-Subtitles-and-Closed-Captions-so-high-</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Another confusing situation for viewers is when they choose both #subtitling and #dubbing in the same language. Unless they know about constraints (lip synch, time and space) they don't understand why the translation is so different. And it can be VERY different</p>
{% endraw %}

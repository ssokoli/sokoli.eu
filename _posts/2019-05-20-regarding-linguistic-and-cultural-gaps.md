<p>Regarding linguistic and cultural gaps. Lets take three languages Spanish, English &amp; Greek and put them in pairs. EN-ES pair is linguistically closer. ES-EL, culturally closer. Guess what happens when ES is translated in EL using EN as a pivot language. <a href="https://twitter.com/translationtalk/status/1130473053111164930">https://twitter.com/translationtalk/status/1130473053111164930</a></p>

<p>I've been reviewing EL subs of ES shows based on EN templates. EN, being culturally further away, would "drag" the EL translation with it. When a literal translation of many ES expressions into EL would work so much better. Shame the Greek subtitlers didn't speak Spanish</p>

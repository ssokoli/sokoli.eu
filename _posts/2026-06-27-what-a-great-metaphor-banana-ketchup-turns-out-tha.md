<p>What a great metaphor: banana ketchup. Turns out that people complain if they ask for ketchup and get banana ketchup. "What's this weird taste on my fries?" So this can be one important reason to stick to the old recipe: serving your customers the thing they asked and paid for.</p>
<p>Then again, some people love banana ketchup and ask for it specifically. If you can make both ketchup and banana ketchup, and serve it accordingly, your business will be OK.</p>
<p>It's all about expectations, right?</p>

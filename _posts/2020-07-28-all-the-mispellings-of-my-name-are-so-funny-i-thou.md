<p>All the mispellings of my name are so funny, I thought they deserve a thread. First things first, I sincerely thank each and every person that tries to write it, no matter the outcome.  And some etymology: #Σταυρούλα is the female version of Σταύρος, which nowadays means cross.</p>
{% endraw %}

<p>The word comes from the verb ίστημι= straigthen up or stand. The Latin equivalent is staurare.  <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stauros">https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stauros</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>And now to the mispellings. Some of them have a different meaning in Greek:<br>
Stravoula = crοoked/cockeyed<br>
Savroula = small lizard<br>
Tavroula = small bull (female actually)<br>
Savvoula = another name, female of Savvas</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>Some people, wanting to sound friendly (I reckon), try to abbreviate it. So today I got:<br>
Hi Starv</p>

<p>A question for my colleagues about the process they follow when #subtitling.</p>
<p>Training courses often recommend watching the content first and then start subtitling. But some colleagues prefer to start subtitling right from the beginning, watching small parts of the material.</p>
<p>In my experience, the approach does not impact the final quality, as long as there are multiple watch-throughs (at least three). It seems more like a matter of personal preference.</p>
<p>I'm curious, what's your usual method?</p>

<p>How easy it is to explain failure and put the blame on "trained translators, who underestimate the domain" and "linguists, who stick to the source text".</p>
<p>How about looking at root causes instead? Could be systemic, like working conditions, deadlines, rates, lack of access to context/purpose/audiences - or could be individual, like ignorance, greed, desperation. (Well, greed goes on all sides actually).</p>
<p>So, next time we feel the urge to quickly put everyone in the same bucket, let's qualify the noun with a "some": *Some* translators do this, *sometimes* linguists do the other. It's the least we can do if we don't have access or the time to look underneath the hood.</p>

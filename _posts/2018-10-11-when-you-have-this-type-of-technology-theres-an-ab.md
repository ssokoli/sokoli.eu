<p>“When you have this type of technology, there’s an ability to do second screen [synchronization], where what the consumer sees, because of the watermark and because you can detect it, you can automatically interact with the content”</p>
<p>Such tracking and ability to synchronise assets would also have great applications in expanding the provision of accessibility services or audiovisual localisation in other languages by making use of the audience's personal devices in venues where currently such services are not available.</p>

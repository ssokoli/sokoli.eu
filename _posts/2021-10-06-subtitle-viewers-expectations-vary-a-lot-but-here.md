<p>#Subtitle viewers' expectations vary a lot, but here are some extremes:<br>
There's the foreign language speaker/learner who wants a literal translation of every word spoken. Feels cheated if "Good morning" is translated as "Hello". Aka the "That's not what he said" type.</p>

{% raw %}
<p>On the other end, the viewer with zero knowledge of the language, big fan of the series, who would do anything to watch it. Even with unedited MT.</p>

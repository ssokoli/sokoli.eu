<p>Today I had the chance to present an old project that is very dear to me, and I am still so proud of it: ClipFlair, #ForeignLanguageLearning through #Captioning and #Revoicing of Clips.</p>
<p>We created the platform more than a decade ago, and despite the technology becoming obsolete (Silverlight is no longer supported) it still looks great, and it's a trove full of inspiring activities based on #AudiovisualTranslation and #MediaAccessibility.</p>
<p>Kudoz to the whole team of experts involved, including Patrick Zabalbeascoa, Thanasis Hadzilacos, Agnieszka Szarkowska, Olga Torres Hostench, Jorge Díaz-Cintas, Lupe Romero, Sara Rovira-Esteva, Anabel Galan, Lucía Molina, Marga Navarrete, Laura McLoughlin, Jennifer Lertola, Cristina Varga, Iratxe Menchaca, among others, and most importantly, our software architect George Birbilis. What a team and such good times together!</p>
<p>I hope we can find a bit of funding some day to do the necessary conversions so that it's properly available again. Btw, it is still accessible if you do the workarounds suggested by George (see first comment)</p>

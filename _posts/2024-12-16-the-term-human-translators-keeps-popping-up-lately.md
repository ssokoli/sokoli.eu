<p>The term “human translators” keeps popping up lately, especially with the recent human vs. AI slams.<br>
But do we really need the specifier? Aren't translators human by default? Does it protect against being replaced by machines, or does it undermine the profession?<br>
What do you think?</p>
<p>A bit more about the options:<br>
- No way! Translators are human by default. Specifying is redundant and demeaning.<br>
- It’s necessary to specify. Like it or not, we need to distinguish humans from machines.<br>
- It could actually be smart marketing. “Human translator” highlights quality, like a human-made seal.</p>

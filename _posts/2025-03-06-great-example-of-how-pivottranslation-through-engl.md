<p>Great example of how #pivottranslation through English can water things down. Let's take an example from Turkish into Greek. The phrase Ağzına sağlık means "Well said!", which could be naturally translated into Greek as Σωστά τα λες. But if we consider its literal meaning, "Health to your mouth", the perfect Greek equivalent would be Γεια στο στόμα σου - a much more idiomatic and expressive choice.</p>
<p>In cases where no Turkish to Greek subtitlers are available, a good English pivot template providing also verbatim translations can certainly help.</p>

<p>Excited to share some great news: The first articles of our special issue in #Perspectives are out! The issue, titled "Pivot Audiovisual Translation: A Burning Issue for Research and Training," is something we had the pleasure of guest-editing with Hanna Pieta, Susana Valdez and Rita Menezes.</p>
<p>In this issue, we explore the challenges and new directions in indirect #AVT, a field that's becoming increasingly important. It's been a rewarding experience working on this, and I believe the articles offer valuable insights for both researchers and practitioners.</p>
<p>Check out the links in the comments for the first available articles:</p>
<p>👉 Using annotated pivot templates to transfer culture specific references in audio description: translators’ performance, strategies, and attitudes by Anna Jankowska</p>
<p>👉 Creativity in pivot audiovisual translation. Parasite subtitled and dubbed in Italian by Margherita Dore.</p>
<p>👉 AD in China at the crossroads: a set of guidelines for localising AD scripts from Spanish into Chinese by Yuchen Liu, Helena Casas-Tost y Sara Rovira-Esteva</p>
<p>👉 Indirect translation in advertising: the case of Estrella Damm by Montse Corrius and Eva Espasa Borrás.</p>
<p>Many thanks to our fantastic peer reviewers, you know who you are 😍</p>
<p>Looking forward to hearing what you all think!</p>

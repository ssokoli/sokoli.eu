<p>Great article on #dubbing process and some of the challenges in #SquidGame.<br>
One of the voice actors is very pessimistic about the future of this work:</p>
<p>“I would say dubbing has two years on the outside and then it’s gone forever,” King declares. “ #AI is going to get rid of it.” He foresees a scenario where technology allows the audience to see an actor’s original performance, just in different languages. “It’ll be him, and it’ll match one-to-one. That will be here within two years. Hands down. I’m very sad that another acting job is going to be gone.”</p>
<p>Of course, I am also highlighting a mention to #dubtitles :)</p>
<p>"Dubbing has become a real priority for Netflix in recent years, a commitment that even extends to the on-screen closed captioning: In the past, if you were watching a dub of a show with the English subtitles on, you’d frequently hear lines of dialogue that didn’t match with the on-screen text — the caption would be phrased or translated in a different way from the audio version. But now, Netflix will switch to an entirely different set of subtitles if you’re listening to the dubbed version, which will align with the spoken English you hear."</p>

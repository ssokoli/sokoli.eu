<p>You know all those #promptengineering courses? By the time they are made they are already obsolete.</p>
<p>The latest OpenAI course on the latest model (o1) teaches us the latest principles of prompting reasoning models:</p>
<p>-Be simple and direct.<br>
-No explicit chain-of-thought required (thought you had learned something? Needless now 😅 )<br>
-Use structure.<br>
-Show rather than tell (no more begging and/or cheering and thanking?)<br>
-Use cheaper models for certain things (optimizing costs becomes and even higher priority).<br>
-Meta-prompting: use o1 to improve your prompts. (no more prompt engineering courses needed?)</p>
<p>PS: OpenAI's courses are free and available at DeepLearning.ai<br>
PS2: This is tongue-in-cheek. I'm not saying one should stop taking such courses. Knowing how the tech works is paramount.</p>

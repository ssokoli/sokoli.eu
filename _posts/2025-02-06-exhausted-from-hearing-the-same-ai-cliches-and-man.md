<p>Exhausted from hearing the same AI clichés and mantras on repeat:<br>
- Automation will take over tedious tasks, freeing us for more creative work.<br>
- AI won’t replace humans, but humans using AI will replace those who don’t.<br>
- AI is just a tool, it all depends on how we use it.<br>
- AI will handle the boring stuff so we can focus on what really matters.<br>
- We’re not replacing jobs, we’re augmenting human capabilities.<br>
- The future of work is human + AI collaboration.</p>
<p>OK, we’ve got it. And yes, there are lots of asterisks to the above. But can we move on to more juicy discussions?</p>

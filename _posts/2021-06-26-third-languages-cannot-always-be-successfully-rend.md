<p>Third languages cannot always be successfully rendered in interlingual #subtitling using current norms. <br>
Case in point:<br>
In #TheFamilyMan (S02E01 00:45) an agent from Mumbai arrives in a Tamil-speaking area. He gives orders to the local agent who translates them to the team (in L3</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>This L3 utterance is meant to be understood by the audience, but not by the Mumbai agent. <br>
However, the foreign audience cannot discern that there are actually two different languages spoken (L1 and L3), so this dialogue must be perplexing:</p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>-[Mumbai agent to local agent in L1]: Follow my lead.<br>
-[Local agent to team in L3]: The guy is an idiot.<br>
-[Mumbai agent in L1]: What did you tell your men just now?<br>
-[Local agent in L1]: I translated your orders</p>
{% endraw %}

<p>Just read that "Large language models speak 320 languages. They think in one" What? No way.</p>
<p>Thanks, AI people, for taking the time to talk about the multilingual that needs to be multicultural, but please oh please, don't spread misinformation, no matter how catchy it is.</p>
<p>LLMs do not internally operate in a language at all. They're all about vector spaces (embeddings). Tokens from different languages are mapped into numerical representations. This is no language, no interlingua.</p>

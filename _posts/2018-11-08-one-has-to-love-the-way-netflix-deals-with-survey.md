<p>One has to love the way #Netflix deals with survey results: A survey conducted by Netflix showed a high percentage of US viewers don't want to watch content in languages other than English. "It's a super depressing  result, so we ignored that survey." <br>
<a href="https://www.cnet.com/google-amp/news/netflixs-plan-to-get-everyone-watching-foreign-language-content">https://www.cnet.com/google-amp/news/netflixs-plan-to-get-everyone-watching-foreign-language-content</a></p>
{% endraw %}

{% raw %}
<p>The company experimented by showing US viewers foreign-language content. If the quality of the content was high, viewers were more than happy to watch it. #Netflix also discovered they were much more likely to finish a show if it had been #dubbed, rather than #subtitled.</p>

<p>Languages &amp; The Media is where academics, researchers, practitioners, language service providers, technology partners and content owners have met for thirty years. Many friendships started at this conference, mine included.</p>
<p>This year was my busiest and most intense conference experience. It included sharing the stage as a speaker, with a talk on how the conference’s discourse has evolved since 1996, and chairing the Workflows &amp; Technology session. And something I had never done before: I represented delsur., an Argentinian language service company. It was exciting, and I hope I helped bring together people who need excellent services with those who can offer them.</p>
<p>The Jan Ivarsson Award went to Pablo Romero-Fresco in recognition of his invaluable contribution to accessible filmmaking and creative accessibility, and I had the honour of handing it to him on behalf of ESIST - European Association for Studies in Screen Translation</p>
<p>Another emotional moment was Yota Georgakopoulou's closing speech, especially when when she talked about αιέν αριστεύειν, "ever to excel", a line from the Iliad. The same words are on the award my son received last year for good grades (and I have a couple of them myself).</p>
<p>Ancient Greek in London, and then the big surprise: the next Languages &amp; The Media will be in Athens.</p>
<p>See you there!</p>

<p>The same person can be a great fan of AI, and also a fervent advocate for human creativity, as well as a promoter of AI-safe spaces.</p>
<p>You can appreciate AI’s capabilities and also believe in the irreplaceable value of humans. And you can promote AI-safe spaces, not as a rejection of AI, but as a way to help human expression and cognitive skills not to be drowned out in the flood of generated content. These positions aren’t mutually exclusive.</p>
<p>This is for all my good friends, especially creators and translators who wouldn’t touch GenAI tools and chatbots with a wooden stick.</p>
<p> #AIsafespaces #pdohumanity by Emily Diamandopoulou</p>

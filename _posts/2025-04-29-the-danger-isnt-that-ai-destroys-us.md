<p>"The danger isn’t that AI destroys us. It’s that it drives us insane"</p>
<p>I was astonished when I first read this more than two years ago. Since then, I’ve gone back to this idea every once in a while. The discussion has centered on AI replacing humans, taking their jobs, and on environmental and ethical issues. Most of them are not new and they happen with other technologies and human interventions on our little planet.</p>
<p>But I think it's going to increasingly shift toward AIs impact on society. And due to its nature and sheer speed is much harder to predict or learn from history.<br>

<p>Andrej Karpathy shares tips on using language models to understand the outputs of language models, since "we'll be spending a lot more time" doing this.</p>
<p>I hadn't heard of ASD-STE100, the controlled language specification he mentions. I found out it was designed to make technical English readable for non-native speakers and machines.</p>
<p>His advice, basically: ask AI to produce different kinds of explainers, like plain-language versions, diagrams, videos, and web pages you can discard after use. Single-use, like plastic cups.</p>
<p>It looks useful and practical. But I wonder: does it lead to understanding, or to the illusion of understanding? And does that understanding last? If we rely on disposable explainers, who will still read and write complex texts? Does that matter?</p>

<p>That's a refreshingly clear and well-argued position. I hope my kids get to have teachers like Prof Maja Korica. What she wants students to learn can only be done with pen and paper. I also hope they have teachers who can teach them when and how to use tools. Not to be faster, more effective, more productive, but to be able to do things that would be impossible without these tools. And others that teach them how to do things without any tools, no pen and paper, just thinking, speaking, debating.</p>

<p>Dear AVT researchers, journalists, and anyone it may concern:<br>
Our AVT world urgently needs more research on the impact of translation quality and creativity on the international success of audiovisual works.</p>
<p>At Language and the Media 2024, Paweł Aleksandrowicz presented a fascinating example: a Turkish series that achieved only mediocre ratings in its home country, but became a hit in Poland - thanks to the exceptionally well-written and creative Polish translation, which reportedly improved upon the original script.</p>
<p>This is an underexplored intersection between translation studies and media reception, that could reshape how we understand the value and authorship of translated audiovisual content.</p>

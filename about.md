I am a researcher, a subtitler and a teacher. I live in Patras, Greece.

I am a senior researcher at the Computer Technology Institute and Press "Diophantus" (CTI), where I have coordinated research projects since 2011. Since 2025 I have also been doing postdoctoral research at the Hellenic Open University on artificial intelligence in education and audiovisual creation. I want to know what these tools make possible, what they replace, and what happens to professional judgement when people use them.

My first research question came from subtitling. Subtitlers leave words out all the time, and I wanted to know which words, and why. The answer became my PhD thesis at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (2011), which compares what film subtitles omit in Greece and in Spain. I have been interested in what subtitles leave out ever since.

During the same years I studied subtitling as a way of learning languages. I coordinated two European projects, Learning via Subtitling and ClipFlair, in which students practise a foreign language by subtitling and revoicing video clips. Later I studied how films with more than one language are translated.

I started translating in 1995 and worked as an interpreter from 1998 to 2008. Since 2001 I have subtitled films and series into Greek, from English, Spanish, Italian, Catalan and German. Since 2016 I have also been Greek Language Lead for subtitling at Deluxe. I still subtitle, and I still find research questions in that work.

I teach Spanish at the Hellenic Open University. I have taught subtitling and translation technology at the Universitat Pompeu Fabra, the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and other universities. I studied psychology in the 1990s, and I am now reading current research on cognition, learning and attention.

I hold a PhD and an MA in Translation and Interpretation from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, and a BA in English Language and Literature from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. I am Vice President of the European Association for Studies in Screen Translation (ESIST) and a member of the AI Localization Think Tank.

At the cinema I stay until the end of the credits, to read the subtitler's name.

I am a senior researcher at the **Computer Technology Institute and Press “Diophantus” (CTI)** in Greece. My research began with subtitling norms and developed into work on audiovisual translation, accessibility, language learning and educational technology. I have coordinated or participated in national and European research projects and published in translation studies and education.

I hold a **PhD** and an **MA in Translation and Interpretation** from the **Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona**, and a **BA in English Language and Literature** from the **Aristotle University of Thessaloniki**. My doctoral thesis, *Subtitling Norms in Greece and Spain: A Comparative Descriptive Study on Film Subtitle Omission and Distribution*, was supervised by Patrick Zabalbeascoa and defended in 2011. I also studied psychology in the 1990s, and I am now updating that knowledge with current research on cognition, learning and attention.

I have taught at the **Hellenic Open University**, **Universitat Pompeu Fabra**, **Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona**, and other institutions. My teaching connects Spanish language education, audiovisual translation and digital tools.

I serve as **Vice President of the European Association for Studies in Screen Translation (ESIST)** and I am a member of the **AI Localization Think Tank**. I was a founding member of the Women in Localization Greece Chapter (2020–2022), served on the board of the Hellenic Society for Translation Studies (2022–2023) and served as Vice President of the Disciplinary Board of the Panhellenic Association of Translators.

Since 2025 I have been carrying out postdoctoral research at the Hellenic Open University's School of Applied Arts and Sustainable Design, on AI in education and audiovisual creation. I am interested in what technologies enable, what they may displace, and the ways professional judgement and creative agency are valued.

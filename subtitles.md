A selection of the films and series I have subtitled or reviewed in Greek since 2001. The list is not complete. I add titles as I find them in my records.

I work from English, Spanish, Italian, Catalan and German into Greek. I write subtitles, I write subtitles for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers (SDH), and I check other subtitlers' work.

When someone sings on screen, I try to write subtitles that can be sung. I do not know whether viewers sing along in Greek. I do.

The year next to each title is usually the year of the film or series, which is often earlier than the year I subtitled it. Some of these titles are also listed at [Subtle, the Subtitlers' Association](https://subtle-subtitlers.org.uk/author/Stavroula/).

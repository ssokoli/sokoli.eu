My research sits between audiovisual translation and language pedagogy: subtitling as a practice, and subtitling as a way of learning a language. More recently my work has turned to what happens when students use AI tools in design and translation classrooms: what changes in creative agency and control, and what doesn't.

I hold a PhD and an MA in Translation and Interpretation from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, and a BA in English Language and Literature from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki. Since 2025 I am conducting postdoctoral research at the Hellenic Open University's School of Applied Arts and Sustainable Design, on artificial intelligence in education and audiovisual creation. I work as a senior researcher at the Computer Technology Institute (CTI) in Greece, I teach as an adjunct lecturer at the Hellenic Open University, and since 2017 I have been Greek Language Lead for subtitling at Deluxe Media.

Before and alongside all this: technical, legal, and commercial translation from English and Spanish into Greek since 1996, and, between 1998 and 2007, simultaneous interpretation — including for UEFA, Euroleague Basketball, and ministerial meetings of two Spanish EU Council Presidencies.
